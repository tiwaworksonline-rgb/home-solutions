    Better leads. Clearer projects.

    Home Solutions is designed to connect contractors with homeowners who have already described their scope, budget, location and preferences. Contractors will complete onboarding and verification before receiving leads.

    How the marketplace will work

    Homeowner submits project → opts into contractor contact → qualified professionals review → quotes and messaging → booking → verified completion → two-way reviews.

    • Service areas and trade specialties
    • Licensing and insurance checks where applicable
    • Quote and project messaging
    • Lead and booking activity dashboard
    • Reviews tied to completed work

    Homeowners stay free

    Our proposed business model combines affiliate revenue with contractor subscriptions and completed-job success fees. No homeowner subscription is planned.

    Contractor pricing is a proposed beta model, not a live offer. No charges or subscriptions are enabled.
